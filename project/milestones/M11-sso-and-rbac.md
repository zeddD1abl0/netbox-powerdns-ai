# M11: SSO and RBAC

## Goal

Sign in through the organization's identity provider, and control who can do what.

## Scope (provisional)

- OIDC, SAML and trusted proxy headers, with Authentik as the reference IdP (Q-028)
- Built-in and custom roles, IdP group mapping, and permissions checked per action (Q-030), scoped by server group (Q-015)
- The generated permission reference
- SCIM provisioning (Q-032)

starts. The milestone's branch is `m11-sso-and-rbac` (ADR-0010).
